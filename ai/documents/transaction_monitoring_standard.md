# Transaction Monitoring Alert Standard

Eldar Northstar Community Bank monitors customer and account activity for patterns that
may be inconsistent with the customer's profile or may indicate money laundering,
fraud, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or misuse of bank products.

## Patterns requiring review

Investigators should review, in context, activity that includes:

- rapid movement of funds into and out of an account with little retained balance;
- repeated transactions just below an internal or regulatory reporting threshold;
- a sudden increase in transaction count or value without a known business reason;
- many unrelated senders paying one beneficiary, or one sender paying many newly
  created beneficiaries;
- repeated round-number transfers or circular movement between connected accounts;
- transfers involving jurisdictions inconsistent with the customer's profile; and
- dormant accounts that resume with high-value or high-frequency activity.

No single rule proves suspicious activity. The investigator must evaluate the
pattern, customer history, account age, counterparties, timing, and any reasonable
economic purpose.

## Alert triage

Alerts are prioritized as critical, high, standard, or low. Critical alerts and
credible sanctions matches must be escalated immediately. High-priority alerts
must receive initial review within one business day. Standard alerts must receive
initial review within three business days.

## Minimum evidence

The case record should identify the relevant transactions, total value, time
window, direction of funds, counterparties, countries, triggered patterns, and
comparison with the customer's expected activity. Automated risk scores may guide
triage but must not replace investigator judgment.
