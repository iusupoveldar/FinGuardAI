# Company and Product Profile

Eldar Northstar Community Bank is a fictional Canadian retail and small-business bank
used for FinGuardAI development. It serves individuals and privately owned
businesses through online banking and a small branch network in Ontario, British
Columbia, and Alberta.

## Products

Eldar Northstar offers personal chequing and savings accounts, small-business operating
accounts, domestic electronic transfers, international wires, debit cards, and
term deposits. It does not offer anonymous accounts, payable-through accounts,
cryptocurrency custody, correspondent banking, or services for shell banks.

## Typical activity

Personal accounts usually receive payroll, benefit, or household transfers and
are used for bills, purchases, and savings. Small-business accounts commonly
receive customer payments and pay suppliers, payroll, taxes, and operating costs.
International wires are available but are not expected for most personal
customers unless documented during onboarding or a later profile update.

## Compliance ownership

The Financial Crime Compliance team owns customer risk policy and transaction
monitoring. Operations analysts perform first-line alert review. AML investigators
handle escalated cases, and the Money Laundering Reporting Officer makes external
reporting decisions. Final decisions must be attributable to a human reviewer.
