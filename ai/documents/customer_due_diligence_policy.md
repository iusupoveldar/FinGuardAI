# Customer Due Diligence and Risk Classification Policy

Eldar Northstar Community Bank assigns every customer an initial risk rating during
onboarding and reviews that rating when customer information or activity changes.
The rating is an internal control and must not be disclosed to the customer.

## Required customer information

The onboarding team records the customer's legal name, date of birth or business
registration date, residential or operating address, occupation or industry,
expected account use, expected monthly transaction volume, and source of funds.
Identity must be verified using approved independent documents or data sources.

## Risk factors

The following factors increase customer risk:

- residence, incorporation, counterparties, or regular activity in a
  high-risk jurisdiction;
- cash-intensive or opaque business activity;
- use of nominees, unexplained intermediaries, or complex ownership structures;
- transaction volume or value materially above the customer's stated profile;
- adverse media, sanctions exposure, or politically exposed person status; and
- repeated incomplete, inconsistent, or expired identification information.

Customers are classified as low, standard, high, or prohibited risk. High-risk
customers require enhanced due diligence and approval from a compliance manager.
Prohibited customers must not be onboarded or provided new products.

## Review frequency

Low-risk customers are reviewed every 36 months, standard-risk customers every
24 months, and high-risk customers every 12 months. A material alert, ownership
change, sanctions match, or unexplained change in behaviour triggers an immediate
event-driven review regardless of the scheduled date.

## Investigation expectations

An investigator must compare observed activity with the documented purpose of
the account, expected transaction levels, known source of funds, and relevant
counterparty locations. Missing profile information is an evidence gap; it is not
by itself proof of suspicious activity.
