# AML Investigation and Escalation Playbook

This playbook defines the minimum steps for reviewing a transaction-monitoring
alert at Eldar Northstar Community Bank.

## Investigation steps

1. Confirm the customer and all accounts included in the alert.
2. Review the customer profile, risk rating, prior alerts, and due-diligence date.
3. Establish the review period and summarize incoming and outgoing activity.
4. Identify material counterparties, countries, transaction types, and recurring
   timing or value patterns.
5. Compare the activity with expected account use and documented source of funds.
6. Record supporting and contradicting evidence and identify missing information.
7. Decide whether to close, request information, continue monitoring, or escalate.

## Decision guidance

An alert may be closed when the activity has a documented, plausible economic
purpose and no material unexplained risk remains. The rationale must cite specific
evidence rather than only an automated score.

Escalate to the AML investigations manager when activity has no reasonable
explanation, involves material high-risk jurisdiction exposure, resembles
structuring or layering, contains inconsistent customer information, or continues
after a prior warning or information request.

Potential sanctions matches must be escalated to the sanctions team immediately.
Employees must not tell a customer that a suspicious transaction report is being
considered or filed.

## Case note format

Every recommendation must contain:

- a concise activity summary and review period;
- risk factors and relevant policy or monitoring rules;
- transaction-level evidence and source references;
- uncertainties, data gaps, and reasonable alternative explanations; and
- recommended next steps, owner, and target completion date.

AI-generated summaries are drafts. A trained investigator remains responsible for
checking evidence, correcting errors, and approving every final decision.
